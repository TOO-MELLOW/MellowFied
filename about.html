About MellowTech

We're more than just technicians — we're your neighbours who love tech and people.

How It All Started

Founded in 2015 by two friends who shared a passion for technology and community, MellowTech began as a tiny repair shop in Observatory, Cape Town. We believed that tech support should be friendly, transparent, and stress‑free — not intimidating or full of jargon.

Over the years we've grown, but our core values haven't changed. We still greet every customer with a smile, explain things in plain language, and treat your devices as if they were our own. Whether you're a student needing help with an assignment, a professional needing a CV revamp, or a business needing a full digital setup — we're here for you.

"We don't just fix things. We make technology work for you."

Our Core Values

Integrity

Honest diagnoses, fair pricing. We never recommend repairs or services you don't need.

Warmth

No intimidating tech speak. We make you feel comfortable, informed, and respected.

Expertise

Continuous learning, certified technicians, and years of hands-on experience across all our services.
